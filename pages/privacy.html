Privacy Policy & Data Governance

Last Updated: September 2026 • Compliance Version 3.1

Protected Health Information (PHI) Notice

FirstChild operates under strict HIPAA standards and international medical privacy frameworks. All clinical files, lab diagnostics, and secure messages are encrypted via AES-256 architecture.

01. Information Categories Collected

To facilitate precision reproductive care, genetic screening, and treatment tracking, we gather specific data categories:

  • Direct Identifiers: Full legal name, date of birth, government-issued identification numbers, contact phone, and residential address.
  • Clinical & Laboratory Records: Ovarian reserve markers (AMH), hormone assays (Estradiol, LH, Progesterone), ultrasound imaging notes, and PGT-A genetic reports.
  • Financial & Insurance Data: PPO/HMO policy numbers, billing statements, out-of-pocket ledger tracking, and HSA/FSA transaction details.
  • Digital Telemetry: Portal login session metadata, browser type, IP address, and encrypted local storage tokens for interface preferences.

02. Use of Cryopreservation & Genetic Data

Data generated within our ISO-5 cleanrooms—including gamete vitrification logs, embryo biopsy genetic sequencing, and donor matching profiles—is stored in segregated, air-gapped clinical databases. This data is utilized strictly for medical identification, chain-of-custody verification, and authorized treatment execution.

03. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosures

FirstChild never sells or leases patient records. Information is shared exclusively with:

  • Accredited Laboratories: Certified genetic testing partners executing PGT-M or PGT-SR chromosomal panels.
  • Insurance Underwriters: Entities verifying pre-authorization for stimulation medication bundles and surgical retrievals.
  • Statutory Health Bodies: Regulatory agencies requiring anonymized success rate auditing (e.g., SART reporting).

04. Medical Record Retention & Deletion Policy

In accordance with medical board regulations and fertility statutes, clinical health records are retained for a minimum of ten (10) years following the final patient interaction, or until minor dependents reach the age of majority. Patients may request electronic archive exports via the secure messaging portal.

05. Patient Privacy Rights & Controls

Patients maintain absolute authority to review their records, restrict specific communication channels, or request corrections to demographic entries. Requests are processed by our compliance bureau within 15 business days.
