02. Cryopreservation & Embryo Storage Agreements

Patients utilizing our liquid nitrogen cryogenic bank for egg, sperm, or embryo storage are subject to distinct Storage Agreements:

  • Annual Fees: Cryostorage invoices are billed annually. Failure to maintain payment or update contact info following notice may designate specimens as abandoned.
  • Facility Transfer: Transfer of genetic material to external clinics requires verified notarized release documentation and clearance of active lab balances.

03. Genetic Screening & PGT Limitations

Preimplantation Genetic Testing (PGT-A/M/SR) provides high-accuracy chromosomal screening but does not guarantee absolute detection of all genetic anomalies, mosaicism limitations, or live birth outcomes. Genetic counseling sessions are mandatory prior to initiating screening protocols.

04. Telehealth & Medication Compliance

Remote consultations and asynchronous chat interfaces supplement in-clinic care. Patients undergoing active ovarian stimulation are strictly bound to follow precise evening dosage timings prescribed in their portal medication dashboard. FirstChild assumes no liability for missed doses or unverified modifications to hormone injection schedules.

05. Financial Estimates & Insurance Mandates

Treatment package estimates reflect projected procedural costs. Patients remain financially responsible for out-of-pocket deductibles, specialty pharmacy injectable top-ups, and unforeseen extended monitoring requirements.
