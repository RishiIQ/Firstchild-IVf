Frequently Asked Contact Questions

Details regarding record transfers, appointments, and out-of-town patient logistics.

How do I securely transfer medical records from my previous fertility clinic?

You can request our HIPAA-compliant records release form directly through the secure messaging portal or email our records department at records@firstchildfertility.com.

Do you accommodate travel schedules for out-of-state or international patients?

Yes. Our concierge travel team coordinates remote follicle monitoring at certified clinics near your hometown, requiring travel to New York only for egg retrieval and embryo transfer.

What should I bring to my initial consultation appointment?

Please bring government photo identification, current medical insurance cards, and any recent pelvic ultrasounds, AMH blood tests, or semen analysis documentation completed within the last 12 months.
