Less Invasive

IUI

Intrauterine Insemination — A gentle, fast-track fertility treatment.

Patient-First Protocol

Streamlined Ovulation & Timing

A less invasive path involving precision sperm processing and timed placement. Ideal for specific male-factor infertility or unexplained cases with high ovulatory reserve. Each cycle is monitored carefully with ultrasound and hormone blood work to optimize timing.

Best For: Mild male factor infertility, unexplained infertility, cervical mucus issues, or same-sex couples using donor sperm.

01
Monitoring

Ultrasound tracking of follicle development and ovulation timing.

02
Sperm Processing

Advanced sperm washing to select the healthiest, most motile sperm.

03
Insemination

Quick, painless placement of prepared sperm directly into the uterus.

04
Pregnancy Test

Blood test approximately 12-14 days after insemination.

IUI Success Factors

  • Age: Best outcomes under 40
  • Ovarian Reserve: AMH > 1.0 ng/mL
  • Sperm Health: Count > 10M motile
  • Tubes: At least one open tube

Cycle Options

  • Natural cycle IUI
  • Clomid-stimulated IUI
  • Letrozole-stimulated IUI
  • Gonadotropin-stimulated IUI
Precision Micro-Injection

ICSI

Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection — The ultimate solution for male factor infertility.

High-Magnification Precision

92% Fertilization Benchmark

Direct sperm injection into the egg using ultra-fine micro-manipulators under high magnification. Our specialized andrology lab maintains an exceptional 92% fertilization rate with the ICSI technique. This is especially effective for severe male factor infertility, previous fertilization failure, or when using surgically retrieved sperm.

Advanced: Combined with PGT-A for comprehensive genetic screening. Ideal for patients with a history of fertilization failure.

01
Sperm Retrieval

Extraction via ejaculation, TESE, or PESA for obstructive cases.

02
Sperm Selection

High-magnification selection of the most morphologically normal sperm.

03
Micro-Injection

Single sperm injected directly into the egg cytoplasm using micromanipulation.

04
Embryo Culture

Fertilized eggs cultured in time-lapse incubators to blastocyst stage.

When Is ICSI Recommended?

  • Severe male factor infertility
  • Previous failed fertilization
  • Surgically retrieved sperm
  • Genetic testing indications
  • Unexplained infertility

Key Facts

  • Fertilization Rate: 92%
  • Pregnancy Rate: 55-65% per transfer
  • Procedure Time: 20-30 minutes
  • Recovery: Same day discharge

Genetic Screening

PGT Screening

Preimplantation Genetic Testing — Comprehensive chromosomal analysis before embryo transfer.

Next-Generation Sequencing

70% Lower Miscarriage Risk

PGT-A (Preimplantation Genetic Testing for Aneuploidy) screens embryos for chromosomal abnormalities before transfer. This advanced genetic testing can improve implantation rates, reduce miscarriage risk, and help select the healthiest embryo for transfer. PGT-SR is also available for patients with structural chromosomal rearrangements.

Key Benefit: Reduces miscarriage risk by up to 70% and increases live birth rates per transfer. Particularly recommended for patients over 37 or those with recurrent pregnancy loss.

01
Biopsy

5-10 cells removed from the trophectoderm (future placenta) on day 5-6.

02
Analysis

Comprehensive chromosomal analysis using next-generation sequencing (NGS).

03
Results

Results in 7-10 days. Embryos are frozen while awaiting results.

04
Transfer

Only chromosomally normal (euploid) embryos are selected for transfer.

Types of PGT

  • PGT-A: Aneuploidy screening
  • PGT-M: Single gene disorders
  • PGT-SR: Structural rearrangements

Who Should Consider PGT?

  • Women 37 years and older
  • Recurrent pregnancy loss
  • Previous chromosomally abnormal pregnancy
  • Family history of genetic disorders
