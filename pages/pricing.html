Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Pricing Questions

Everything you need to know about cycle fees, medication estimates, and refunds.

Are stimulation medications included in the procedural package?

Stimulation injectables are billed separately by accredited specialty pharmacies because dosages depend dynamically on individual ovarian reserve markers (AMH) and daily ultrasound responses.

What is our financial policy if a cycle is canceled before egg retrieval?

If stimulation is stopped early due to poor follicle recruitment or hyperstimulation risks, you are only billed for monitoring sessions completed to date, and all unused procedural balances are refunded immediately.

Who qualifies for the Multi-Cycle Refund Guarantee?

Enrollment requires a comprehensive medical evaluation, AMH baseline evaluation, and physician review to ensure clinical protocol alignment before starting.
